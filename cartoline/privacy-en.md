# Cartoline privacy policy



Last updated: 2 November 2026

## Who we are

Cartoline is a block puzzle game made by Itsukii Studio.
Data controller: **Emanuele Pina (Itsukii Studio)**.
For any question about your data write to: **itsukii-dev@outlook.it**.

## In short

- There is no account. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number.
- Games, scores, settings and reminders are saved on your phone. **We never receive them.** They may end up in your phone's backup, if you have it on.
- The game is free thanks to advertising. Ads are provided by Google (AdMob) and use some data, described below.
- The RevenueCat service manages purchases and starts every time you open the app, even if you buy nothing. A purchase, if any, goes through Apple or Google.
- We do not sell your data.

## What stays on your phone

Games in progress, scores, the postcard album, day streak, the sound, music and vibration switches and reminders are saved in the phone's memory. So are a few notes the app needs to work: how many sessions and games you have played (to space out the ads), whether you removed the ads, and the last settings file downloaded. The language follows the phone's language.
They are never sent to us. They can, however, be copied into the phone's backup (for example iCloud on iPhone or the Android backup), if you have it on: that copy is managed by Apple or Google, not by us.


The daily puzzle reminder is a notification scheduled by the phone itself. It is off by default and you turn it on. It sends nothing to anyone.


If you press "Share", a short text goes to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp): your score, for the daily puzzle its date and your day streak, and a link to download Cartoline. Cartoline does not send it to anyone else.


## Advertising

Ads do not appear in your first play session, and in that session Google's advertising service is not started at all. From the second session on ads can appear.


To show them we use Google AdMob. According to Google's documentation (version 25.5.0 for Android) and the privacy declaration included in Google's software for iPhone (version 13.10.0), AdMob can receive:

- the phone's IP address, from which an approximate location can be estimated (not your precise location);
- how you use the app: launches, taps, ads viewed;
- technical information about how it runs (start-up speed, hangs, energy use);
- on Android, the advertising ID, an app identifier and other identifiers linked to accounts on the phone, if any. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings; the app identifier you cannot;
- on iPhone, the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you give permission, and also other identifiers tied to the app or to the developer, which Google may receive even if you refuse the permission.

Google uses this data to show ads, for statistics and to prevent fraud. Google may share it with other advertising companies. The list of these companies is in Google's consent form described below. Google handles its data under its own policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy


**Your consent.** In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google shows you a form where you choose how your data is used for ads. On your phone you first see a short Cartoline screen that explains it.


On iPhone, the phone asks whether you allow Cartoline to "track" you for ads. If you refuse, ads still appear, without the IDFA, and the game does not change.


You can change your mind at any time: open **Settings**, section **Ads and privacy**, then **Privacy choices** (shown where your country requires it). On iPhone you can also change the permission in the phone's settings.


About ad content: we ask Google for ads suitable for most audiences (PG rating) and we block some categories (for example gambling and dating).


## Purchases

"Remove ads" is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. You pay Apple or Google, who tell us that the purchase happened: the product bought, the date, the price and the store country. We do not see your card details.
It removes the ads between games. The optional videos stay available, so Google's advertising service keeps working in the app even after the purchase.
To recognise the purchase (even if you change phone and press "Restore purchases") we use **RevenueCat**, a service that handles purchases on our behalf. RevenueCat starts every time you open the app, even if you buy nothing. It receives an anonymous random code linked to the app, the language and currency of your store, on iPhone your answer to the tracking question described above, and, if you buy, your purchase history. It does not link the code to your name.


## Remote settings file

So that we can adjust ad frequency and difficulty in the future, the app downloads a small settings file from our website (itsukii-dev.github.io), normally once every 12 hours; if the download fails, it tries again later.
The app sends no identifier. The site is hosted by GitHub (GitHub Pages), which records the IP address of every visit for security reasons. GitHub's privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement
Without a connection the app uses the settings it already has and works the same.


## Other

- **No account, no sale.** We do not sell your data. The advertising partners named above (Google and the companies Google shares data with) receive the data described to show ads.
- **Children.** Cartoline is not made for children and is not intended for anyone under 14.
- **How long.** Data on the phone stays until you delete it or uninstall the app; any copies in the phone's backup follow Apple's or Google's rules. For data received by Google, RevenueCat and GitHub the retention periods in their own policies apply (Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy; RevenueCat: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy; GitHub: link above).
- **Legal bases we rely on.** Your consent for personalised ads and, on iPhone, for the IDFA (you can withdraw it at any time, as explained above); the purchase contract to recognise and restore "Remove ads"; legitimate interest for non-personalised ads, fraud prevention and the remote settings file.

## Your rights

In the European Union you have the right to know what data we hold about you, to ask for it to be corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict a use, to receive it in a readable format and to withdraw consent. Write to **itsukii-dev@outlook.it**.
If you think your data is not handled properly you can contact the **Garante per la protezione dei dati personali** (Italy): https://www.garanteprivacy.it/home/diritti/come-agire-per-tutelare-i-tuoi-dati-personali


## Changes

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top. If the change is important we also say so prominently at the start of this page.
